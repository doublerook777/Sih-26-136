THIS INNOVATION PILOT IMPLEMENTATION AGREEMENT is entered into under the SIH 26136 Public Procurement Innovation Framework between the Contracting Government Authority and the Selected Startup Entity.

Clause 1: Parties to the Agreement

This Agreement is executed between the {{ challenge.department | default('Department of Urban Development') }}, representing the Government of the State / Municipal Authority ("Authority"), and {{ startup.name | default(pilot.startup_name | default('AquaSense Systems')) }}, a DPIIT-recognized startup incorporated under Indian laws ("Startup Partner").

Clause 2: Scope of Pilot Work

The Startup Partner shall deploy, operate, and calibrate its innovative technology solution to address the administrative bottleneck articulated under Challenge CHAL-{{ challenge.id | default(pilot.challenge_id | default('001')) }} ({{ challenge.title | default('Smart Municipal Water Leak Detection') }}) in the designated pilot area: {{ pilot.location | default('District A') }}.

Clause 3: Deliverables & Specifications

The Startup Partner shall deliver: (a) Hardware/software integration with existing municipal telemetry, (b) Real-time analytical dashboard access for designated department officers, (c) Operational field telemetry for the duration of {{ pilot.duration_days | default(90) }} days, and (d) Final verified performance and impact report.

Clause 4: Pilot Timeline & Phasing

The total duration of the innovation pilot is {{ pilot.duration_days | default(90) }} days from the Effective Date, structured strictly across four sequential delivery milestones.

Clause 5: Milestone Schedule & Financial Allocation

Disbursements shall be released tranche-by-tranche strictly upon verified completion of milestones. Total milestone allocations sum exactly to the pilot budget of ₹{{ "{:,}".format(pilot.budget | default(1000000)) }}.

Clause 6: Payment Terms & Verification Conditions

No tranche shall be disbursed without an affirmative approved verdict from an empanelled Independent Validator. Payments shall be released within 5 business days of validation via digital escrow protocol.

Clause 7: Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Pilot success is benchmarked against pre-agreed quantifiable metrics across Technical, Cost, Impact, and Scalability categories. Performance achievement shall be capped at 1.2x per indicator to prevent statistical skew.

Clause 8: Data Ownership & Sovereignty

All municipal operational data, citizen telemetry, and sensor logs generated during the pilot remain the exclusive, sovereign property of the Contracting Authority.

Clause 9: Intellectual Property (IP) Rights

Pre-existing background IP remains the property of the Startup Partner. The Authority receives an irrevocable, royalty-free, perpetual license to use the pilot outputs for municipal administration.

Clause 10: Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

Both parties agree to protect all sensitive infrastructure diagrams, network topologies, and proprietary algorithms from third-party disclosure.

Clause 11: Cybersecurity & Baseline Compliance

The Startup Partner warrants continuous compliance with the 8-point cybersecurity baseline, including end-to-end payload encryption (AES-256), TLS 1.3 API endpoints, and immutable access audit logging.

Clause 12: Risk Management & Mitigation Framework

All identified field risks must be recorded in the Pilot Risk Register with designated mitigation owners. Any high-risk trigger (Score > 15) necessitates an immediate joint remediation review.

Clause 13: Liability & Indemnification

The Startup Partner indemnifies the Authority against third-party intellectual property infringement claims arising directly from the pilot deployment.

Clause 14: Independent Milestone Validation Protocol

An independent technical validator shall physically and digitally verify claimed field metrics prior to tranche release.

Clause 15: Early Termination & Dispute Resolution

The Authority may terminate the agreement upon 14 days written notice if two consecutive milestones fail validation or if a critical security breach occurs.

Clause 16: Scale-Up Procurement & Replication Conditions

Upon achieving a composite final performance score of ≥ 85% (SCALE), the Startup Partner qualifies for streamlined procurement across adjacent municipal districts under GFR 2017 innovation provisions.
