1. Purpose & Scoring Framework

This document sets out the transparent, multi-expert evaluation rubric used by empanelled independent technical and domain experts to assess shortlisted startup applications. Each expert independently evaluates shortlisted proposals on a 0–100 scale across seven standardized dimensions. The final evaluation score is computed as the weighted arithmetic mean across all assigned experts.

2. Standardized Evaluation Criteria & Percentage Weights

Scoring weights are calibrated for this challenge category to reflect domain-specific priorities (e.g., technical feasibility, scalability, and security posture). All weights strictly sum to 100%.

3. Consensus & Weight Snapshot Policy

Auditability & Defense: When an expert submits scores, the active rubric weights are permanently frozen onto the evaluation record (rubric_snapshot_json). Any subsequent modification to the rubric creates a new immutable version without retroactively altering existing evaluations.
